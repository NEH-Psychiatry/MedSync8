# Wisconsin — key points for the telepsychiatry assistant

Two public policy documents in this folder. Neither contains patient information.

## ForwardHealth Update 2026-07 (March 2026)
- G0511 and G0512 end-dated 2025-12-31. Community health centers bill Psychiatric CoCM with the CPT codes under Online Handbook topic #22557 and general BHI under topic #23217.
- The interactive maximum allowable fee schedule on the ForwardHealth Portal is the authority for allowable rendering provider types and fees; policy is subject to change.
- Claims suspended for the delayed 2026 code implementation are processed automatically; reconcile remittances before any rebilling.
- Documentation retention: Wis. Admin. Code § DHS 106.02(9); Wis. Stat. § 49.45(3)(f).
- Managed care organizations must provide at least the fee-for-service benefit.

## 2025 Wisconsin Act 17 — APRN Modernization (effective 2026-09-01)
- APNP certification is replaced by an APRN license; valid APNP certificates convert automatically on 2026-09-01.
- Independent practice requires 3,840 documented RN-level hours over at least 24 months plus 3,840 APRN-level hours over at least 24 months with a physician or dentist immediately available, then Board authorization. CNMs are exempt from the hours pathway.
- Collaborative agreements are no longer required by state law once independent authorization is granted, but employers may still require them, and invasive pain-syndrome treatment still requires a pain-medicine physician agreement.
- Renewal is biennial with 16 CE contact hours in pharmacology or therapeutics, including 2 on responsible controlled-substance prescribing. Malpractice coverage at IPFCF minimums is mandatory; independents outside an employment relationship enroll in the IPFCF.
- The Act changes state scope-of-practice law only. Medicare, Medicaid (ForwardHealth), and private payor billing rules are unchanged; independent authority does not by itself confer independent billing.
