# ForwardHealth Update No. 2026-07 (March 2026) — CPT, HCPCS, and CDT Procedure Code Changes Effective January 1, 2026

Source: Wisconsin Department of Health Services, ForwardHealth Update 2026-07, publication P-1250, March 2026.
Affected programs: BadgerCare Plus, Medicaid. Addressed to all providers, HMOs and other managed care programs.
Published in accordance with Wis. Stat. § 49.45(2). Public policy document; no patient information. Text transcribed from the official PDF for retrieval; the PDF governs where they differ.

## Procedure code changes

Effective for dates of service on and after January 1, 2026, ForwardHealth updated covered services, policies, and service limitations to reflect the 2026 first-quarter CPT, HCPCS, and CDT procedure code changes (additions, revisions, and deletions) for BadgerCare Plus and Wisconsin Medicaid.

ForwardHealth adopted updates to CPT, HCPCS, and CDT procedure codes for these services: audiology; dental; disposable medical supplies; medicine; radiology; remote physiological monitoring evaluation and management services; surgery.

## Community health center behavioral health codes end-dated

On December 31, 2025, ForwardHealth end-dated two HCPCS procedure codes (G0511 and G0512) used by community health centers (CHCs) for behavioral health services.

- CHCs must use the CPT procedure codes listed in the ForwardHealth Online Handbook Collaborative Care Model topic #22557 and follow the policy when submitting claims for Psychiatric Collaborative Care Management services.
- CHCs must use the CPT procedure code listed in the Behavioral Health Integration topic #23217 and follow the policy when submitting claims for general behavioral health integration services.

Refer to the interactive fee schedules on the ForwardHealth Portal for complete information on general ForwardHealth coverage related to the updated codes, including allowable rendering provider types and maximum allowable fees. Policy information for CPT, HCPCS, and CDT procedure codes is subject to change; the interactive fee schedules and the Online Handbook carry the most current policy and coverage information.

## Suspended claims now processing

ForwardHealth's implementation of 2026 CPT, HCPCS, and CDT procedure codes was delayed. ForwardHealth temporarily suspended processing claims with certain 2026 procedure codes submitted on and after January 1, 2026. ForwardHealth has completed the systems updates and will automatically process these suspended claims. No provider action is required.

## Documentation retention

Providers must follow the documentation retention requirements of Wis. Admin. Code § DHS 106.02(9) and produce or submit documentation to the Wisconsin Department of Health Services (DHS) upon request. Per Wis. Stat. § 49.45(3)(f), providers shall maintain records as required by DHS for verification of provider claims for reimbursement. DHS may audit such records to verify actual provision of services and the appropriateness and accuracy of claims, and may deny or recoup payment for services that fail to meet these requirements. Refusal to produce documentation may result in denial of submitted claims, recoupment of paid claims, application of intermediate sanctions, or termination from the Medicaid program.

## Managed care organizations

This Update applies to service areas that members receive on a fee-for-service basis and through BadgerCare Plus, Medicaid SSI, and other managed care programs. For managed care implementation of the updated policy, contact the appropriate managed care organization (MCO). MCOs are required to provide at least the same benefits as those provided under fee-for-service arrangements.
