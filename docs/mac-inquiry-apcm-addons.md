## Letter

To: Provider Contact Center — Written Inquiries
Re: Concurrent reporting rules, edits, and allowed amounts for HCPCS G0568, G0569, and G0570 (Advanced Primary Care Management behavioral health add-ons), effective January 1, 2026

Nuestra Esperanza Health furnishes psychiatric Collaborative Care Model (CoCM) and general Behavioral Health Integration (BHI) services to Medicare Part B beneficiaries, some of whom also receive Advanced Primary Care Management (APCM; HCPCS G0556, G0557, G0558) from the same practitioner. The CY2026 Physician Fee Schedule final rule created HCPCS G0568, G0569, and G0570 as optional APCM add-on codes valued by direct crosswalk to CPT 99492, 99493, and 99484. We have placed these codes on an internal billing hold and request written guidance on the following questions before any claim is submitted.

### Question 1 — Add-on versus time-based codes in the same month

For a Part B patient receiving APCM (G0556, G0557, or G0558), when the same practitioner reports G0568, G0569, or G0570 in a calendar month, may that practitioner **also** separately report a time-based CoCM or BHI code — CPT 99492, 99493, 99494, HCPCS G2214, or CPT 99484 — for the same patient in the same month? Or is the add-on code to be reported **in lieu of** the time-based code for that month? Please cite the controlling authority.

> **Authority attached:** CY2026 PFS final rule, 90 FR 49266, §II.G "Enhanced Care Management" (CMS-1832-F); CMS MLN909432 *Behavioral Health Integration Services* (January 2026), p. 13; CMS MLN Matters MM14315 / CR 14315 (Transmittal R13507CP).
> **Why we ask:** CMS finalized the add-ons as "directly comparable to existing CoCM and BHI codes" with a 1:1 RVU crosswalk but did not print an operative sentence on concurrent reporting. NACHC's APCM Reimbursement Tip Sheet (March 2026) states that BHI/CoCM furnished in conjunction with APCM "must be billed using the appropriate APCM behavioral health integration add-on code(s)" and separately flags the point as "CMS Clarification Pending."

### Question 2 — NCCI edits

Are there active National Correct Coding Initiative Procedure-to-Procedure (PTP) edits or Medically Unlikely Edit (MUE) values pairing G0568, G0569, or G0570 with CPT 99492, 99493, 99494, HCPCS G2214, or CPT 99484? If a PTP edit exists, please state the Column 1 / Column 2 assignment and the modifier indicator (0 or 1).

> **Authority attached:** CMS NCCI Practitioner PTP and MUE quarterly data files (versions effective January 1, April 1, July 1, and October 1, 2026).
> **Why we ask:** We located no such edit in the published quarterly files, but absence of a located edit is not clearance, and the codes are new for 2026.

### Question 3 — Allowed amounts and base-code prerequisite

Please confirm (a) the CY2026 allowed amounts your jurisdiction pays for G0568, G0569, and G0570 (non-facility, by locality), and (b) that these add-on codes are payable only when an APCM base code (G0556, G0557, or G0558) is present on an accepted claim for the same patient, same practitioner, and same calendar month.

> **Authority attached:** CMS Physician Fee Schedule Look-Up Tool (CY2026); CMS-1832-F finalized RVU crosswalk (G0568←99492, G0569←99493, G0570←99484); CMS MLN909432 (January 2026) descriptors ("list separately in addition to the Advanced Primary Care Management code").
> **Our working figures (estimation-only, national non-facility):** G0568 ≈ $161.66, G0569 ≈ $145.96, G0570 ≈ $57.78, per the CMS-derived NACHC tip sheet. We will replace these with your jurisdiction's locality amounts.

### Question 4 — G2214 in an APCM month

May HCPCS G2214 (CoCM/BHI shorter-service month, ≥30 minutes) be reported for an APCM-enrolled patient in a month in which the practitioner reports G0568 or G0569 — or in a month in which the CoCM base-code minimum was not met and no add-on is reported? Stated plainly: is G2214 reportable **alongside**, **instead of**, or **never with** G0568/G0569 for an APCM patient?

> **Authority attached:** CMS MLN909432 (January 2026) G2214 descriptor; CMS-1832-F §II.G.
> **Why we ask:** This is the one relationship our billing rules cannot encode from any primary source. Neither MLN909432 nor the final rule addresses G2214 in the APCM context.

### Question 5 — Care-team structure under 42 CFR §410.26(b)(5)

Do G0568, G0569, and G0570 require the full role-based CoCM care-team structure (designated behavioral health care manager plus psychiatric consultant, with registry, validated rating scales, and systematic caseload review) as required for stand-alone CPT 99492/99493? Or, because CMS finalized these add-ons as designated care management services under 42 CFR §410.26(b)(5), may qualifying activities be furnished by auxiliary personnel under general supervision?

> **Authority attached:** 42 CFR §410.26(b)(5); CMS-1832-F §II.G; NACHC APCM Reimbursement Tip Sheet (March 2026), "CMS Clarification Pending" note.
> **Why we ask:** CMS described the add-ons using activity language drawn from the CoCM/BHI models while also designating them care management services, and did not reconcile the two in the final rule.

### Question 6 — FQHC claim construction (FQHC servicing MAC only)

For an FQHC furnishing APCM, please confirm the payment mechanism and claim construction for G0568, G0569, and G0570: that they are paid at the PFS national non-facility rate as care coordination services separate from the PPS encounter; the correct claim format when a PPS encounter and the add-on appear on the same claim; and the applicable coinsurance and QMB handling.

> **Authority attached:** CY2026 PFS final rule §III.B (RHC/FQHC care management); NACHC APCM Reimbursement Tip Sheet (March 2026).

We would appreciate a written response citing the controlling authority for each answer. Thank you.

Codie Jordan Vassar, MD, MS
Medical Director, Nuestra Esperanza Health
